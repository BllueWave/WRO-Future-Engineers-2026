# Vehicle photos

Rule 7 (p.9 of the 2026 rules) asks for photos of the vehicle from every side, from the top and from the bottom, and for a team photo. Each view has its own file:

| View | File | What the photo shows |
|---|---|---|
| Front | `Vehicle_Photos/front.jpg` | Nose, front HC-SR04, both slanted corner sonars |
| Back | `Vehicle_Photos/back.jpg` | Rear of the body, drive side |
| Left | `Vehicle_Photos/left.jpg` | Full left side, wheels and Pixy2 mount |
| Right | `Vehicle_Photos/right.jpg` | Full right side |
| Top | `Vehicle_Photos/top.jpg` | Controller, driver, wiring, power and start switches |
| Bottom | `Vehicle_Photos/bottom.jpg` | Chassis plate, drive axle, steering linkage |
| Team | `docs/team_photos/team_photo.jpg` | Fawaz Alasousi and Dawood AlEneezi |

Every vehicle photo shows the car in its competition configuration, battery fitted and body on. All six use the same background and light, with the camera square to each side.
