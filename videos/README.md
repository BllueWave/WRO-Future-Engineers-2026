# Videos

Rule 7 (p.9 of the 2026 rules) asks for one YouTube video per challenge, public or unlisted, in which the part showing autonomous driving lasts at least 30 s. A video stored only in this folder does not meet the rule; the YouTube link is what counts.

## Current links (June 2026)

These two clips were published in June 2026, during the national-round season. They show the car on the code of that time, before the `open_v20` and `obstacle_v20` sketches now in [`src/`](../src/README.md).

| Challenge | YouTube | Local copy | Clip length | Autonomous driving |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open Challenge | https://youtube.com/shorts/_rmwh_EwI1A | [`openChallenge.mp4`](openChallenge.mp4) | 46.8 s | about 39 s (a hand places the car for the first 7 s) |
| Obstacle Challenge | https://youtube.com/shorts/2quu5O000I0 | [`Obstacle_Challenge.mp4`](Obstacle_Challenge.mp4) | 37.0 s | about 32 s; the clip ends while the car is still driving, no parking shown |

The lengths were measured on the local copies. Both clips pass the 30 s rule, the Obstacle one by about 2 s.
